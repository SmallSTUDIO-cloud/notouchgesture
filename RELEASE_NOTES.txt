Aerviqo 0.5.0

Aerviqo is the renamed and extended PalmLink visual build. The original screenshot/transfer architecture remains the baseline.

Included:
- supplied Aerviqo logo artwork without redesign;
- automatic Gallery save to Pictures/Aerviqo;
- shutter sound ON/OFF;
- screenshot shutter and blue-edge visual feedback;
- send/receive preview animation;
- faster temporal hand tracking and confidence-dropout tolerance;
- separate Settings place;
- illustrated in-app gesture manual;
- optional scroll, swipe, volume, brightness, cursor/select, zoom, Back/Home and media controls;
- optional shake flashlight;
- experimental foreground mouth-open Air Mic;
- Nearby recovery for already-connected (8003) and out-of-order (8009) races.

The final Android build and physical-device validation must be performed in the configured Codespace/Freebuff environment.
